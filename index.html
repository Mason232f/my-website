Gas Leak in Popular Cave, Closed off

Locals Report

Group of 20 tourist visiting a cave, faint after a pipe in the cave burst. Members of the group fainted besides 2 who escaped and called local authorities. Local Authorities also shut off the cave to the public to undergo some repairs and implement more inspections to prevent further incidents.

Authorities Statement

Cave will undergo some investigations to ensure the safety of the public, "Cave will be closed off for major repairs of pipes and structural supports" the goverment has also stated that the cave will be open for sure once inspections deemed cave "safe".

Protesters Against Reopening

Protesters stand seek to cancelation of reopening the cave in fear of more people being harmed in future related incidents.

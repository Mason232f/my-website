Asian Cuisines

Top 3 Asian Cuisines to try and enjoy! rated by Mason Meas

Butter Pound Cake

Pound Cake Recipe

Butter Pound Cake is a good dish to try if you have
a sweet tooth. This sweet and warm desert taste like
wafles but more enjoyable. There is no need for
maple syrup!

Pho

Pho Recipe

Pho is a traditional soup/noodles from South East Asia, Vietnam.
The dish is good for when you are sick or simply wanting to enjoy
a warm dish with your family. This is a perfect meal to have during
Winter. This dish can have spice added into the dish to
match everyones preference.

Cambodian Egg Rolls

Cambodian Egg Roll Recipe

Cambodian Egg Rolls are a great traditional dish too have at functions and gatherings. This dish is commonly eaten with
sweet sour sauce. The dish is usually made with egg yolk and little meat packed with vegetables (like carrots and cabbage) inside a
wheat or flour based wrap. you fry them after creating unfried rolls of joys. Amazing to go snack when completed but time consuming to
prepare a large amount. Best cooking with one or multiple other people to save time.

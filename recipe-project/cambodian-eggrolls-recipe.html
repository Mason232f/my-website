Cambodian Eggrolls

Ingredients needed:

  • 1 lb of ground pork or beef
  • 2 cups of cabbage, shredded
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 yellow onion, chopped finely
  • 1 cup of bean sprouts
  • 1 cup of carrots, shredded
  • 2 cups of vermicelli rice nooodles (soaked in hot water, then cut into 2 inches in length)
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 3 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon of flour
  • 1 tablespoon of soy sauce
  • 1/4 teaspoon of fresh black pepper
  • 32 ounces egg roll wraps (2 packages)
  • oil (for deep frying)
  • 1 teaspoon of chicke bouillon
  • 3 tablespoons of oyster sauce

Vietnamese Pho

Ingredients needed:

  • 3 lb of brisket or 2 lb of meaty bones or 2 lb of marrow bones
  • Ginger
  • Onion
  • Cinnamon
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom
  • Coriander
  • Star Anise
  • Fish Sauce
  • Salt
  • Limes
  • Hoisin Sauce and Sriracha or other spices to serve dish with.

Method to cook:

  1. Quick boil - remove impurities from the beef and is the path for a beautiful clear soup.
  2. Scum - Remove any icky stuff in the soup
  3. Wash - Wash the bones to remove the Scum
  4. Simmer for 3 hours - The bones, beef, ginger, onion, and other spices need to settle in the soup.
  5. Remove the brisket - Some is used as Pho topping.
  6. Simmer 40 minutes - Just with the bones this time.
  7. Strain
  8. Ladle into the bowls over boiled noodles and enjoy your meal!

Butter Pound Cake

Ingredients needed for this recipe includes:

  • 200 gm cake flour
  • 50 ml milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 170 gm caster sugar
  • 200 gm unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 3 eggs

Method of cooking:

  1. Let the butter sit at room temperature until softened and prepare the oven to 365F.
  2. Sift the cake with the baking powder and salt. Set it aside and line the pan with
    baking paper or spray the bottom and side of it with oil. Beat the eggs and drain
    through a fine sieve to remove any foam.
  3. Beat the butter in a bowl with an electric mixer at low speed until it is smooth.
    Add sugar and beat at medium speed. Then increase the mixer to high speed and continue
    that until very pale and creamy.
  4. Add the beaten eggs and beat well after each addition of the eggs. Beat in the
    vanilla extract.
  5. Sif in the flour mixture, in 3 batches, then gently fold to combine with a spatula,
    scraping down the bowls sides occasionally.
  6. Add milk and quickly combine it into a smooth batter. Make sure to not over mix
    to prevent your cake turning tough. Scrape the batter into the lined pan and level the surface
    Quickly after, transfer it into the preheated oven and bake for about 20 mins.
